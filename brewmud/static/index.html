How to play BrewMUD

Explore

Each location lists its residents and exits. Move with N, S, E, W, U, D, I, or O. Use MAP when you lose your bearings.

Shorten names

You rarely need to type a full name. TALK TRAIN works for the Training Coordinator, and L SENSOR works like LOOK DISSOLVED-OXYGEN SENSOR. Capitalization does not matter.

Take assignments

Talk to brewery residents; many will ask for help. Your current objectives appear with room descriptions. Use JOURNAL for details or HINT for directions.

Work together

Use WHO to find other players and FOLLOW to travel together. After joining a group, type ordinary conversation in the group> box. Pop quizzes are individual, but discussion is encouraged.

Begin by entering TALK TRAIN when you reach the brewery.
