How to play BrewMUD

Explore

Each location lists its residents and exits. Move with N, S, E, W, U, D, I, or O. Use MAP when you lose your bearings.

Shorten names

You rarely need to type a full name. TALK TRAIN works for the Training Coordinator, and L SENSOR works like LOOK DISSOLVED-OXYGEN SENSOR. Capitalization does not matter.

Take assignments

Talk to brewery residents; many will ask for help. Your current objective appears in the side panel. Use JOURNAL for details or HINT for directions.

Work together

Use WHO to see other players. When someone is in your location, enter SAY hello in the command box to speak to them. Players explore independently, and pop quizzes remain individual.

Begin by entering TALK TRAIN when you reach the brewery.
