How to play BrewMUD

Explore

Click an exit on the picture to move, an object to examine it, or a Talk button to meet a resident. The story appears below the picture; conversations and object details also appear on it. Click an information button again, the caption, or × to close it. You can always switch to Classic text if you prefer typing commands.

Check your knowledge

Pop quizzes have clickable answers. If you need to review, click Pause, then Notes or explore the area. Click Resume quiz when ready. After a correct answer, click Continue to reveal the room.

Take assignments

Talk to brewery residents; many will ask for help. Your current objective appears in the side panel. Click Journal for details or Hint for directions.

Work together

Click Who to see other players. Players explore independently, and pop quizzes remain individual. If you want to chat, switch to Classic text and use SAY.

At the brewery gate, click Talk to Training Coordinator to begin.
