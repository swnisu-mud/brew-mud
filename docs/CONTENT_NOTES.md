# Content notes

The initial brewery content was adapted from the canonical, deduplicated BBMB 1200 slide set extracted locally. It emphasizes malting and barley, brewing water, starch and mash enzymes, brewhouse operations, yeast metabolism, fermentation flavors, hops, lightstruck chemistry, carbonation, foam, packaging, sanitation, and beer styles.

The history lecture and accidental iBEST PDFs are intentionally excluded from the game.

The world is educationally simplified but avoids several common misleading shortcuts: oxygen is needed chiefly before fermentation for yeast membrane-lipid synthesis; alkalinity and hardness are distinct; alpha- and beta-amylase have different cleavage patterns and temperature stability; late oxygen and early wort oxygen have different consequences; H2S and DMS are not treated as the same sulfur fault; IBU is not presented as a direct measurement of perceived bitterness; and cleaning is distinguished from sanitizing.

Quest actions are fictionalized for play—enzymes and small molecules speak and may be escorted—but their biochemical explanations and process relationships are intended to remain accurate to the course material.
